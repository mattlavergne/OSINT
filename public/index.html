GhostTrace

Passive OSINT console

Three lookups, public sources only

Phone

Validates against Google's libphonenumber rules, then resolves line type, the geographic area the range is allocated to, the original carrier, and every timezone the number could sit in.

Domain

RDAP registration and registrar, full DNS record set, SPF/DMARC/MTA-STS posture, subdomains from Certificate Transparency, and the security headers the site actually serves.

IP

Geolocation with an honest precision label, RDAP netblock ownership and abuse contact, reverse DNS, plus open ports and known CVEs from Shodan's free InternetDB.

Everything here reads public registries, public DNS and public certificate logs. Nothing is scanned, brute-forced or enumerated against a target, and no lookup is performed on your behalf at a third party that would attribute the request to you. Use it on infrastructure you own or are authorised to assess.
