GhostTrace

Passive OSINT console

Six lookups, public sources only

Phone

libphonenumber validation and line type, then the North American numbering plan's own record: rate centre, the physical switch the block homes on, the operating company, and whether that company is a wholesale carrier selling disposable numbers. Business attribution from OpenStreetMap, SEC filings and Wikipedia.

Email

Gravatar profile and the accounts its owner verified, GitHub accounts publishing the address, mail deliverability and provider, domain age, and an honest classification: organisation, consumer, role account, alias or throwaway.

Username

Account discovery across platforms with real existence APIs — never a guess at an HTTP status. Names, locations, join dates and cryptographic account proofs are correlated so you can see whether the hits are one person or several.

Domain

RDAP registration, full DNS, SPF/DMARC/DKIM and the sending platforms behind them, Certificate Transparency, technology fingerprinting, favicon hash for origin discovery, subdomain mapping with takeover and origin-leak detection.

IP

Geolocation across four providers with their disagreement measured, the operator's own RFC 8805 geofeed where one exists, BGP routing and history, DNS blocklists, Tor directory status, and exposure from Shodan's free InternetDB.

ASN

Registry record and legal holder, every announced prefix, upstream transit providers, peers, internet-exchange presence, and every published route to a human who can act on abuse.

Everything here reads public registries, public DNS, public certificate logs and documents sites publish at conventional public paths. Nothing is scanned, brute-forced or enumerated against a target, and no lookup is performed on your behalf at a third party that would attribute the request to you. Use it on infrastructure you own or are authorised to assess.
